18. ENTERPRISE DOCUMENT MAPPING & DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT ARCHITECTURE
WHY DOCUMENT MAPPING IS REQUIRED

In cyber investigation systems:

One FIR can have multiple documents
One notice can have multiple attachments
One email reply can contain multiple files
One evidence can relate to multiple notices
One transaction can have multiple bank response PDFs

Therefore enterprise systems NEVER store documents directly in one table.

A centralized document management architecture is required.
